Shape Words: Learn English — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Shape Words does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

Your game progress (level, found words, word book, chosen language and settings) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No in-app purchases
  • No internet connection is required; the app makes no network requests
  • No access to camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected, no data from children is collected either.

Third-party content

Voice recordings were generated with Kokoro (Apache 2.0); pronunciations come from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary; the word filter uses the LDNOOBW list (CC BY 4.0). These are bundled inside the app and do not send any data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
